1. What is confidential?

Confidential Information includes non-public transaction details, amounts, references, account information, payment instructions, calculations, correspondence, documents, and related operational information supplied in connection with the transaction.

2. Your confidentiality obligation

The Receiving Party must use reasonable care to protect Confidential Information and should not voluntarily share it with unrelated third parties. The information may be used only to review, document, investigate, obtain advice about, or complete the relevant transaction.

3. Strict Confidentiality Obligation

The Receiving Party shall maintain all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not voluntarily disclose or make it available to any third party, whether directly or indirectly, without the prior written authorization of the Disclosing Party.

4. Information not covered

The Agreement does not cover information already lawfully known without restriction, information that becomes public without a breach, information lawfully obtained from another source without a confidentiality duty, or information independently developed without using Confidential Information.

5. Duration and scope

These confidentiality terms are intended to apply for three (3) years from the Effective Date, subject to applicable law.
